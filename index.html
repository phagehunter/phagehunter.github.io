About

Biology, culture, and the threads between them.

Pathogens have a knack for disrupting society. It's our job to understand them. The deeper we look, the more we learn about ourselves: our cultures, our fears, our shared history.

Curtis Hoffmann is a researcher working at the intersection of biology and anthropology. He studies how microbes move through communities and cultures, and he designs live outbreak simulations that help people experience how epidemics unfold.
