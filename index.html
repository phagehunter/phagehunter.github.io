Interactive atlases

Six ways of looking at a lineage.

Each of these is a self-contained, static site: open data, one page, no account, built to be explored on a phone as easily as on a desk.

Exoplanet Atlas

Six thousand worlds beyond the Solar System, one sky. Browse 6,278 confirmed planets by size, orbit and host star.

Twenty-Four Cousins

A field guide to the human family: 24 hominin species across seven million years, with a time slider, brain-size chart and sourced profiles.

About

Biology, culture, and the threads between them.

Curtis S. Hoffmann is a researcher at Georgetown University working across infectious disease, virology, network science and medical anthropology.

Published work ranges from a One Health response to severe fever with thrombocytopenia syndrome in Thailand, to transcriptomic biomarkers of severe COVID-19, to epidemiological modeling of Operation Outbreak, a real-life outbreak simulation for students. The atlases above are the other half of the same habit: taking a large public dataset and making it something a person can walk around in.
